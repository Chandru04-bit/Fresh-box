Our Story

Reinventing the Weekly Grocery Ritual

Founded in 2022, FreshBox began with one simple realization: supermarket produce spends up to 7 days in cold storage before reaching your shopping cart, losing up to 40% of its vital nutrients.

We built a direct farm-to-subscriber network that eliminates middlemen warehouses. By aggregating customer orders through weekly subscriptions, we harvest precisely what is needed at sunrise, pack it in biodegradable crates, and deliver it to your doorstep by afternoon.

Our Mission

To empower modern families to eat nutrient-rich, chemical-free food effortlessly, while ensuring independent organic farmers receive fair, predictable weekly prices for their seasonal harvests.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted, zero-waste fresh food subscription network, creating a closed-loop sustainable food system where packaging is returned, soil is regenerated, and health is celebrated.
