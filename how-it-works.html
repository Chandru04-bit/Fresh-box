Cold Chain Assurance

Zero Plastic, 100% Thermal Protection

We use recyclable jute and sugarcane-fiber insulation packs with non-toxic frozen ice gel pods to keep leafy greens crisp and dairy chilled even if you aren't home immediately.

  • Reusable Eco-Totes: Driver swaps your empty tote with the new one weekly.
  • Returnable Glass Bottles: Fresh milk in sterile returnable glass bottles.
  • Contactless Dropoff: Safe doorstep drop with photo confirmation.
